Personal Contributions to the Game

Textured and modeled all the environment assets in the game project. UV maps of the props are grouped together in a 1024 x 1024 map. The props are textured to look rusty and worn down. All assets are low poly to let the custom game engine run smoothly.

Below are the cinematics I rendered in Unreal Engine 5. I did the scene building and setup. Lighting and post-processing were used to enhance the visual appeal and atmosphere of the scenes.
